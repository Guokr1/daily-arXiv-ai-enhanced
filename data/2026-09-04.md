### [1] [Direct Four-Spacecraft Measurement of Reconnection Exhaust Thickness in the Solar Wind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03088)
*Mani K Chettri,Rupak Mukherjee,Hemam D. Singh*

Main category: physics.space-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单航天器是推断太阳风电流片厚度的标准方法，但该方法从未在相同的重联相关穿越事件上直接与多点观测结果进行对比验证。多点观测虽然已测试了通用电流片几何，但仍缺乏针对同一重联穿越事件的直接比较，因此需要填补这一空白。

Method: 利用MMS（磁层多尺度任务）四颗卫星在2017年11月10日原始太阳风中观测到的四个电流片穿越，包括一个已确认重联排气的两个边界和两个邻近电流片。通过磁场的互相关分析获得毫秒级延迟不确定性、4°-8°的边界法线和5%-8%的速度精度，并与三种标准单航天器估计方法进行对比。

Estimating the normal from one spacecraft is the larger source of error, though the thickness is still recovered to within tens of percent when a degenerate minimum-variance solution is rejected. At the actively reconnecting sheet, the boundary speed exceeds the local plasma speed along the normal by $29\% \pm 10\%$.

A simplified analytical description of the errors is also provided and discussed. We demonstrate that, due to averaging over many observations per source, the errors in the astrometric parameters are drastically smaller than the corresponding relativistic effects in individual observations.
