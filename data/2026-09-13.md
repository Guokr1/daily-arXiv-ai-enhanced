### [1] [Electrovacuum Black Hole Uniqueness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11140)
*Qing Han,Marcus Khuri,Gilbert Weinstein,Jingang Xiong*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 在轴对称稳态电真空设定下证明黑洞唯一性猜想，通过奇异调和映射分析，表明多视界的渐近平坦Einstein-Maxwell解要么是Majumdar-Papapetrou解（对数角缺陷为零），要么所有角缺陷严格为负、相互作用力严格吸引。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 证明轴对称、稳态、电真空情形下的黑洞唯一性猜想，推广Kerr唯一性的奇异调和映射方法至Einstein-Maxwell情形。

Method: 对相关奇异调和映射进行精细的渐近分析，包含轴上的解析性假设，扩展[18]中用于真空Kerr唯一性的奇异调和映射方法。

Result: 任何该类渐近平坦Einstein-Maxwell解，若有多于一个黑洞视界分量，则或者经对偶旋转后为Majumdar-Papapetrou解（所有对数角缺陷为零），或者每个有限轴杆对数角缺陷严格为负，从而每个相互作用力严格吸引。

Conclusion: 在多视界情形下证明了黑洞唯一性猜想，得出解必为Majumdar-Papapetrou或相互作用力严格吸引的结论。

Abstract: We prove the black hole uniqueness conjecture in the axially symmetric, stationary, electrovacuum setting, subject to the refined asymptotic analysis of the associated singular harmonic maps, which includes an analyticity hypothesis at the axes. More precisely, it is shown that any asymptotically flat solution of the Einstein--Maxwell equations in this class, with more than one black hole horizon component is either: Majumdar--Papapetrou, up to a duality rotation, in which case all logarithmic angle defects vanish, or every finite axis rod logarithmic angle defect is strictly negative and hence every interaction force is strictly attractive. The proof extends the singular harmonic map method used for vacuum Kerr uniqueness in [18].
